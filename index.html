A Brief History of the Itsekiri People

The Itsekiri (also called Iwere or Jekri) are a unique Nigerian ethnic group from the Niger Delta Delta State, known for their rich monarchy, deep connection to the Benin Kingdom, and centuries-old maritime culture.

Descendants of a royal Benin prince and local leaders, the Itsekiri built one of West Africa's earliest centralized kingdoms with the Olu (King) at its heart — a dynasty that continues today.

Our language, Ọmạtẹ Itsekiri, blends Yoruba roots with Portuguese and English influences from early trade — a living testament to our global history.
