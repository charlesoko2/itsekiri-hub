Our History

The Itsekiri (Iwere) people and the Kingdom of Warri

Origins

The Itsekiri (also called Iwere or Jekri) are an ethnic group of the western Niger Delta in present-day Delta State, Nigeria. According to tradition, the Kingdom of Warri was founded by a prince from the Benin Kingdom who settled among local leaders and became the first Olu.

From that union grew one of the region’s earliest centralized kingdoms, with the Olu at its heart. The institution continues today, and the Olu remains the spiritual and cultural head of the Itsekiri people.

Themes in Our Story

Monarchy

A continuous line of Olus, with ceremonies and titles that still shape community life.

Maritime Culture

Creeks, rivers and the coast made the Itsekiri skilled traders and canoe-folk.

Language

Ọmạtẹ Itsekiri carries Yoruba-related roots and the marks of centuries of trade with Europeans.
